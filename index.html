Built for safe local trading

Everything you need to buy or sell USDT with Birr, in one place.

Escrow on every order

When you start a trade, the amount is reserved on the ad. It is released only after the seller confirms your payment.

Local payment methods

Commercial Bank of Ethiopia, Telebirr, CBE Birr, Bank of Abyssinia, Awash Bank and Dashen Bank.

Your price, your limits

Post an ad with your own rate, minimum and maximum order size, and the payment methods you accept.

Protected accounts

Two-factor authentication (an authenticator app) protects posting ads and updating orders.

How it works

Three steps from offer to USDT in your wallet.

1

Choose an offer

Filter by amount and payment method, then pick the rate you like.

2

Start the trade

Enter how much USDT you want. An escrow order opens and the amount is reserved.

3

Pay and release

The buyer pays and marks the order as paid. The seller checks the money arrived and releases the USDT.

Questions

Quick answers before you start.

What can I trade?

USDT against Ethiopian Birr. Offers are posted by other Etp2p users.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Create an account to see live offers and start trades.

Why do I need an authenticator?

Posting ads and updating an order (marking it paid, releasing or cancelling) need two-factor authentication, so nobody else can act on your behalf.

Is crypto trading risky?

Yes. Prices move and mistakes cannot always be undone. Only trade what you can afford to lose, and release USDT only after the money is in your account.
